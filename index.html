AuraStep

Small Steps, A Better You

Small steps, tracked beautifully — with widgets that keep you moving.

AuraStep is a clean, modern step-counter app for Android that turns your daily walk into a simple, motivating habit. Track steps, distance, and calories burned throughout the day, in a light or dark theme that matches your style.

Its home-screen widgets — available in small, medium, and large sizes — bring your progress straight to your launcher, so you never have to open the app to see how you're doing.

Features

  • 👣 Accurate daily step, distance & calorie tracking
  • ⚡ Real-time or battery-friendly periodic tracking modes
  • 📊 Weekly analytics with trends and goal streaks
  • 🧩 Small, medium & large home-screen widgets
  • 🌞 Light and 🌙 dark themes
  • 🎯 Custom daily step goals
  • 🔔 Milestone and reminder notifications

Every step counts — start building a healthier you, one day at a time.
