AuraStep

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraStep ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. AuraStep is developed by Rohan Krythos ("the Developer"). By using AuraStep, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

AuraStep is designed to work without requiring an account or personal sign-up. The App may access or generate the following data, purely to provide its core features:

  • Step, distance and activity data — read from your device's built-in step-counter sensor (via the Android Activity Recognition permission) to show your daily steps, distance, calories, and active minutes.
  • App preferences — such as your daily step goal, theme, and tracking mode (Real-Time or Periodic), stored only to personalize your experience.
  • Device and crash diagnostics — basic technical information (device model, Android version, app crash logs) collected automatically via Firebase Crashlytics, solely to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location (GPS), contacts, photos, or any other personally identifiable information, and AuraStep does not require you to create an account.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your step and activity history is stored locally on your device only (using an on-device database). It is not uploaded to, or stored on, any server we operate. If you uninstall the App, this locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Activity Recognition / Body Sensors (Step Counter) — required to count your steps.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications — used only when you enable "Real-Time Tracking", to show a persistent, low-priority notification while step tracking runs in the background (this is an Android system requirement for any app running a background service, not a way for us to message you).
  • Boot Completed — used to resume step tracking automatically after your device restarts, if tracking was already active.

4. Third-Party Services

AuraStep uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — used to display ads within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — used for crash reporting and app stability monitoring. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

AuraStep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account or collect personal identifiers of any age group.

6. Data Deletion

Since your step data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the App. There is no server-side account or data for us to delete on your behalf.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
